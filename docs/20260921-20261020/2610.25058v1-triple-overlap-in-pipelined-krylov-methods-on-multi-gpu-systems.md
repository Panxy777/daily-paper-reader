## 摘要
我们提出了一种新颖的三重重叠实现，用于流水线预处理共轭梯度（Pipe-PCG）和流水线稳定双共轭梯度（Pipe-BiCGStab）求解器，这些方法在科学应用中广泛使用。通过利用集合通信库，我们的方法将传统的计算-通信重叠扩展为并发执行集合操作、不规则P2P通信和计算内核，从而在多个GPU上实现计算-通信-通信重叠。我们在MareNostrum 5（H100）和Leonardo（A100）超级计算机上评估该设计，并使用SuiteSparse矩阵和三个扩展至512个GPU的大规模实例，与PETSc以及最先进的双流NCCL实现进行比较。三流设计在每个实例上都优于双流基线，优势从4个GPU时的2-6%扩大到64个GPU时的18-47%。在64个GPU上，对于Pipe-PCG，相对于双流基线的几何平均加速比在MareNostrum 5上为1.33倍，在Leonardo上为1.23倍，而Pipe-BiCGStab达到1.30倍。在大规模实验中，与双流设计相比，我们观察到Pipe-PCG最高加速35%，Pipe-BiCGStab最高加速33%。对于PETSc，优势更大，Pipe-PCG和Pipe-BiCGStab均最高达到3.27倍加速。所提方法的相关代码可在线获取：https://github.com/ParCoreLab/aCG

## Abstract
We introduce a novel triple-overlap implementation of the pipelined preconditioned conjugate gradient (Pipe-PCG) and pipelined stabilized biconjugate gradient (Pipe-BiCGStab) solvers, methods widely used in scientific applications. By leveraging a collective communication library, our approach extends conventional computation-communication overlap to concurrently execute collective operations, irregular P2P communication, and compute kernels, enabling computation-communication-communication overlap across multiple GPUs. We evaluate the design on the MareNostrum 5 (H100) and Leonardo (A100) supercomputers, comparing against PETSc and the state-of-the-art two-stream NCCL implementation using SuiteSparse matrices and three massive instances scaled up to 512 GPUs. The three-stream design outperforms the two-stream baseline on every instance, with the margin widening from 2-6% at 4 GPUs to 18-47% at 64 GPUs. At 64 GPUs, the geometric-mean speedup over the two-stream baseline is 1.33x on MareNostrum 5 and 1.23x on Leonardo for Pipe-PCG, and reaches 1.30x for Pipe-BiCGStab. In large-scale experiments, we observed a speedup up to 35% in Pipe-PCG and up to 33% in Pipe-BiCGStab compared to the two-stream design. For PETSc, the margin is even wider, up to 3.27x speedup in both Pipe-PCG and Pipe-BiCGStab. Related code for the proposed method is available online: https://github.com/ParCoreLab/aCG
