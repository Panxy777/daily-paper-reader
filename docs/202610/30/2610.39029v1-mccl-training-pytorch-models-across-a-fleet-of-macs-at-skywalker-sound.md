## 摘要
Skywalker Sound 是一家顶尖的音频后期制作机构，在 10Gb 以太网上运行着 200 多台 M4 Max Mac Studio。当艺术家不使用时，这些机器处于空闲状态，代表着大量尚未利用的统一内存和计算能力。尽管这些机器集群是与现有 NVIDIA GPU 集群一起进行分布式机器学习的绝佳候选，但 PyTorch 缺乏面向 Apple Metal Performance Shaders（MPS）的加速分布式后端。为弥补这一空白，我们推出 Metal Communication Collective Library（MCCL），这是首个用于交换式以太网上多节点 PyTorch 训练的开源 MPS 后端。我们在 24 个节点上评估其性能和可扩展性，表明大规模 all-reduce 操作可达到 TCP 有效吞吐量的 98–99.7%。训练一个 113.5M 参数的 Vision Transformer（ViT）时，吞吐量从 198 样本/秒扩展到 4,568 样本/秒，实现 95.9% 的并行效率。与 NVIDIA A100 GPU 进行基准比较，一台 M4 Max Mac 的 FP32 矩阵乘法吞吐量约为 A100 的 1.7 倍，而 5.5 台 Mac 在使用 TF32 时可匹敌一块 A100。每晚利用 100 台空闲 Mac 运行 12 小时，每年可抵消约 200,000 美元的等效云计算成本。我们的结果表明，现有 Mac 基础设施可以被无缝重新用于高性能分布式训练，而无需放弃标准 PyTorch 代码库。

## Abstract
Skywalker Sound, a premier audio post-production facility, operates more than 200 M4 Max Mac Studios on a 10Gb Ethernet network. When not in use by artists, these machines sit idle, representing massive untapped unified memory and compute capacity. While these fleets are excellent candidates for distributed machine learning alongside existing NVIDIA GPU clusters, PyTorch lacks an accelerated distributed backend for Apple's Metal Performance Shaders (MPS). To bridge this gap, we introduce the Metal Communication Collective Library (MCCL), the first open-source MPS backend for multi-node PyTorch training over switched Ethernet. We evaluate its performance and scalability across 24 nodes, demonstrating that large all-reduce operations reach 98-99.7% of TCP goodput. Training a 113.5M-parameter Vision Transformer (ViT) scales from 198 to 4,568 samples/s, achieving 95.9% parallel efficiency. Benchmarked against an NVIDIA A100 GPU, one M4 Max Mac equals roughly 1.7x the throughput of FP32 matrix multiplication, and 5.5 Macs match an A100 using TF32. Harnessing 100 idle Macs for 12 hours nightly offsets approximately $200,000 annually in equivalent cloud compute costs. Our results demonstrate that existing Mac infrastructure can be seamlessly repurposed for high performance distributed training without abandoning the standard PyTorch codebase.
