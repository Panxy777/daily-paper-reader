## 摘要
Coflow 刻画了数据并行应用的集合通信需求，良好地调度它们对现代数据中心的性能至关重要。我们研究异构并行网络中的非分裂 Coflow 调度问题，该网络包含 m 个网络核心，这些核心可以是电子分组交换机（EPS）、非全停式光电路交换机（OCS）或全停式 OCS，并且每条流必须完全通过单个核心传输。针对该场景的已有近似算法的保证会随核心数 m 或端口最大流度 τ 线性增长，因此当数据中心部署更多核心时，这些保证会变弱。这种依赖性的来源在于舍入步骤：将每条流舍入到其最大分数值所在的核心，可能会使某个端口的负载膨胀 m 倍。我们用一种迭代松弛舍入方案替代该步骤，该方案同时处理输入端口和输出端口的容量约束。对于最小化完工时间，在纯 EPS、纯非全停式 OCS 和纯全停式 OCS 网络中，该方案分别得到 min{τ,3}、2min{τ,3} 和 4min{τ,3} 的近似比。对于最小化总加权 Coflow 完成时间，在同样的三种架构下，我们分别获得 8min{τ,4}、16min{τ,4} 和 32min{τ,4} 的近似比。在混合网络中，保证由其中存在的最弱架构决定，因此总体近似比分别至多为 4min{τ,3}≤12 和 32min{τ,4}≤128。与异构并行网络中非分裂 Coflow 调度的所有先前保证不同，这些界是绝对常数，不随核心数 m 或流度 τ 增长。我们还证明，底层线性规划的整数性间隙至少为 2。

## Abstract
Coflows capture the collective communication requirements of data-parallel applications, and scheduling them well is essential to the performance of modern data centers. We study non-splitting coflow scheduling in a heterogeneous parallel network whose $m$ network cores may be Electronic Packet Switches (EPS), not-all-stop Optical Circuit Switches (OCS), or all-stop OCS, where every flow must be transmitted entirely through a single core. Previous approximation algorithms for this setting have guarantees that grow linearly with the number of cores $m$ or with the maximum flow degree $τ$ of a port, and therefore become weak as data centers deploy more cores. The source of this dependence is the rounding step: rounding each flow to the core holding its largest fractional value can inflate the load of a port by a factor of $m$. We replace this step with an iterative relaxation rounding scheme that handles the input-port and output-port capacity constraints simultaneously. For minimizing the makespan, this yields approximation ratios of $\min\{τ, 3\}$, $2\min\{τ, 3\}$, and $4\min\{τ, 3\}$ for pure EPS, pure not-all-stop OCS, and pure all-stop OCS networks, respectively. For minimizing the total weighted coflow completion time, we obtain approximation ratios of $8\min\{τ, 4\}$, $16\min\{τ, 4\}$, and $32\min\{τ, 4\}$ for the same three architectures. In a hybrid network, the guarantee is governed by the weakest architecture present, so the overall ratios are at most $4\min\{τ,3\}\le 12$ and $32\min\{τ,4\}\le 128$, respectively. Unlike all previous guarantees for non-splitting coflow scheduling in heterogeneous parallel networks, these bounds are absolute constants that do not grow with the number of cores $m$ or with the flow degree $τ$. We also show that the integrality gap of the underlying linear program is at least $2$.
