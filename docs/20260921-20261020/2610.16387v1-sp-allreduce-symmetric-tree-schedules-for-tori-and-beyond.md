## 摘要
Allreduce 是大规模分布式训练中的基础通信原语，而环面互连仍广泛部署于高性能系统中。我们提出 SP-allreduce，一种针对 $d$ 维环面的新型 allreduce 算法，它利用拓扑的对称性且仅沿最短路径路由。若一个 allreduce 算法没有带宽开销、没有多跳引起的流量膨胀，并实现每轮完美的负载均衡，我们称其为完全带宽最优。SP-allreduce 基于一族经过精心对称化的最短路径树，在 $α$--$β$ 模型中保证完全带宽最优性。我们证明，在 $d$ 维环面上所有完全带宽最优的 allreduce 算法中，它使用最少可能轮数，因此在该类中具有最小运行时间。在此意义上，SP-allreduce 对 Bucket allreduce 进行了改进：它保留了 Bucket 的关键优势，同时将轮数减少一半。我们进一步将方法扩展到更广泛的对称拓扑以及指定深度的通信树。实验上，在测试配置中，所得算法相较于环面上最佳对比的先进算法，在方形环面上最高提升 $1.19\times$，在矩形环面上最高提升 $1.65\times$。在 HyperX 和 spine-leaf 网络上，我们分别展示了相较于最佳评估基线最高 $14.82\times$ 和 $1.72\times$ 的增益。

## Abstract
Allreduce is a fundamental communication primitive in large-scale distributed training, and torus interconnects remain widely deployed in high-performance systems. We introduce SP-allreduce, a new allreduce algorithm for $d$-dimensional tori that exploits the symmetry of the topology and routes only along shortest paths.   We call an allreduce algorithm \emph{fully bandwidth-optimal} if it incurs no bandwidth overhead, no multihop-induced traffic inflation, and achieves perfect per-round load balance. SP-allreduce is based on a family of carefully symmetrized shortest-path trees that guarantees full bandwidth optimality in the $α$--$β$ model. We prove that it uses the minimum possible number of rounds among all fully bandwidth-optimal allreduce algorithms on $d$-dimensional tori, and therefore the minimum running time in this class. In this sense, SP-allreduce refines Bucket allreduce: it preserves Bucket's key strengths while reducing the number of rounds by a factor of two.   We further extend our approach to broader symmetric topologies and to communication trees of prescribed depths. Experimentally, the resulting algorithms improve over the best compared state-of-the-art algorithms for tori by up to $1.19\times$ on square tori and $1.65\times$ on rectangular tori across the tested configurations. On HyperX and spine-leaf networks, we demonstrate gains of up to $14.82\times$ and $1.72\times$, respectively, over the best evaluated baselines.
